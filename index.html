About Me

I help customers find the right materials, tools, and supplies for their creative projects. My role includes providing friendly customer service, organizing craft products, maintaining an orderly shop, and assisting with product selection. I have an interest in arts and crafts, handmade creations, and practical DIY solutions, with a focus on making shopping convenient and enjoyable. Through attentive service, product knowledge, and efficient shop support, I aim to help customers bring their creative ideas to life.
